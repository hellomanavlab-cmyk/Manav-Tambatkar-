My journey.

Not a résumé. A look at how I got here and where I’m heading. It’s still being written.

1 / 9

  1. It started with curiosity.

    My interest in technology and in business started early, and the two kept pulling my attention in the same direction. I wanted to know how things work, and how someone turns an idea into something people use.

  2. Learning the tools.

    I began learning digital skills: design, web basics, and the AI tools that were changing how people work. Every new tool answered one question and opened three more.

  3. Web development and AI.

    Web development and AI became the two areas I kept returning to. I explored how websites are built, how automation connects tools, and how AI can remove repetitive work.

  4. The first projects.

    My first digital projects were small and imperfect. I made them anyway, because building something is the fastest way to understand it.

    Learn by building. Improve by breaking things.

  5. Thinking like a founder.

    Somewhere along the way I stopped asking only “how does this work?” and started asking “what problem could this solve, and for whom?”

  6. The ManavLab vision.

    ManavLab began as a vision: a home for exploring ideas, building projects and developing future innovations. A technology group is a long-term goal, and I’m building towards it step by step.

  7. Developing SkillBridge.

    SkillBridge is where the vision became a working initiative: opportunity research, lead qualification and outreach preparation, built around practical value rather than promises.

  8. Engines and rockets.

    Outside the screen, I’m fascinated by mechanical engineering, automotive innovation, and rockets and space exploration. I’m on the polytechnic route towards engineering, still a student and still learning, and I’m not pretending otherwise.

    Honest about where I am. Clear about where I’m going.

  9. What comes next.

    Grow ManavLab, deepen my engineering foundation, and keep building things that solve real problems. The next chapters are still unwritten.
